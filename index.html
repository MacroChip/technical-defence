THE CONVOY ATTACKS

Somebody bolted a battleship cannon onto a farm tractor, and now there is no going back.

  • 🛻 Buy technicals from the garage — they drive the road on their own and fire at anything fortified.
  • 🏰 The towers are the enemy. Bunkers, watchtowers and mortar pits sit still and shoot back.
  • 💵 Wrecked fortifications pay bounties. Vehicles that survive to the end of the road bring home salvage.
  • ❤️ Every technical you lose costs morale. At zero morale the convoy calls it a day.
  • ⌨️ Hotkeys: 1–7 to deploy, Space to start a wave.
